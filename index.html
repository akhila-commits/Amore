Our Story

Welcome to Amore Kalutara

Located at 250, Main Street, Kalutara South, Amore was created around a single heartfelt belief: that every moment shared with the ones we cherish deserves to be celebrated.

Whether you are savoring artisan Revello chocolates crafted with silky cocoa, enjoying freshly baked pastries with barista coffee, planning a custom tiered celebration cake with Sama Dream Cakes, or reserving our private party venue for family memories, our doors are open with genuine warmth.

Step inside our ambient cafe setting and experience handcrafted culinary artistry right in the heart of Kalutara.
